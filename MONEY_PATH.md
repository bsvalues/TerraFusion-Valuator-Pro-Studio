# Money Path — Valuator Pro

## Buyer
**Certified General (commercial) fee appraiser** — solo and small firms. They already pay for tools and
bill by the hour, so time saved and review-defensibility convert directly to money.

## Job to be done
> "Finish the commercial analysis and a defensible report faster — three approaches, the regression, the
> narrative, the PDF — without losing the evidence trail."

## The path (self-serve, no salesman)

## Pricing (launch/test)
| Plan | Price | For |

| Solo Appraiser | $49/mo | one appraiser |
| Pro Appraiser | $149/mo | higher volume + regression/reconciliation + priority |
| Firm | $399/mo | multiple appraisers, shared workfiles, orders |
| Per-report | $29/report | low-volume / pay-as-you-go |
**Founding access:** first 10 users keep a discounted rate for life.

## First 10 customers (profile)
Commercial fee appraisers in your network and state CG-appraiser circles / appraisal forums who:
- do **commercial** work (not just residential forms),
- feel **time pressure** per assignment, and
- worry about **review/litigation defensibility.**
Get them on a demo assignment first; the hours saved plus evidence trail is the conversion.

## Why this converts
Appraisers buy tools with clear ROI. One assignment's time savings ≥ a month of Solo. The pitch is the
product, not a salesperson.
